  What doesn't work

  1. It reads like a very disciplined student essay, not like a person writing about something they've actually built.

  Every paragraph does exactly one thing. Every transition is perfectly clean. There's zero mess. Real writing from someone who cares about the subject has moments of uneven energy — excitement when
  explaining the mechanism, frustration when discussing the cost, a half-aside that reveals the author's actual opinion. This article is clinically neutral throughout. That neutrality is itself a kind of
  artificiality.

  2. "招式" appears ~7 times. It's being used as a leitmotif — find one metaphor, ride it through the whole piece. A human writer uses it 2-3 times then switches to direct language. By the fifth time it
  reads like a verbal tic.

  3. The opening is the weakest part. "LLM agent 现在的一种典型做法：写一段长系统 prompt，挂一堆工具" — anyone who reads AI articles has seen this setup a hundred times. It's correct and boring. The kernel
  RCA scenario in paragraph 3 is far more interesting. A more human writer would start there — the specific pain, the vivid scenario — and let the general observation emerge from it. Instead we get general
  → specific → general, which is textbook essay structure and exactly how models write.

  4. The Redux/event sourcing analogy is performative. A full paragraph to set up an analogy, then immediately "但这个类比只是锚点." If you're going to dismiss it, compress it to a parenthetical: "（熟悉
  Redux 的读者可以把 agent 理解成 dispatch，harness 理解成 reducer）". Either the analogy illuminates or it doesn't — don't dedicate a paragraph to say "here's an analogy but don't take it seriously."

  6. The closing is theatrical.  "整个 pipeline 不是你要借的东西。要借的是一条设计直觉。" — This creates false drama (don't take X!) to resolve it one sentence later (take Y!). A more confident writer just
  says what you should take away.

  7. The rhythm barely varies. Short declarative. Short declarative. Compound with dash. Short declarative. Repeat for 1400 characters. This is what "write tight" looks like when a model executes it
  mechanically. A real writer's rhythm shifts with the content — denser when explaining mechanism, looser when giving context, sharp when making the point.

  Part 2: Workflow / Skill Review

  What the workflow does well

  - Stage separation is genuinely useful. The pipeline makes sense.
  - Quality gate with specific fallback routes per gate is the best part of the whole system.
  - 反模式 sections in each stage are pragmatic — they catch real mistakes, not theoretical ones.
  - De-ai checklist (模板连接词, 作者动作句, 客服式收尾) targets real signals.
  - "先找阶段，再做当前步" as a core principle prevents the most common LLM writing failure: rushing to draft.

  What should change

  S1. The workflow vocabulary is hardcoded for 品牌稿 but the structure works for tech articles too.

  Framing asks for "钩子" (news hook), "前置卖点" (lead USP), "品牌出场" (brand appearance), "陪衬对象". When we ran it on a tech article, these concepts required constant mental translation. The workflow
  worked, but the friction was unnecessary.

  Suggestion: Add a vocabulary mapping note in 01-framing.md or 00-workflow-overview.md:

  ▎ 技术文章适配：钩子 → 为什么现在写；前置卖点 → 最该被记住的一件事；品牌出场 → 项目/系统首次出现；主推对象 → 核心论点或设计选择

  S2. Evidence-layering (02) is the most valuable stage but the 最短路径 skips it.

  The 最短路径 is framing → drafting → quality-gate → de-ai. This skips 02 entirely. But in the run we just did, the material ledger was the single most useful artifact — it's what prevented mixing code
  facts with CLAUDE.md self-description. The workflow should warn more clearly that skipping 02 is the highest-risk shortcut.

  Suggestion: Add to 00-workflow-overview.md under 最短路径: "跳过 02 的代价：容易把口径混进事实。只在素材来源单一、你已经知道什么能写什么不能写的时候跳。"

  S3. De-ai (07) checks for "too AI" but not for "too anti-AI."

  The de-ai pass on this article changed "退化" to "顶不住" — which is deliberately rougher Chinese. Over-correction is itself a form of AI-ness. It produces writing that's trying too hard to not sound like
   AI. The five-dimension check (Directness, Rhythm, Trust, Authenticity, Density) covers this in theory under "Authenticity" but doesn't call it out explicitly.

  Suggestion: Add to 07-de-ai.md 重点排查项:

  ▎ 7：矫枉过正
  ▎
  ▎ - 为了去书面感，把正常用词换成刻意口语化的替代
  ▎ - 为了避免工整，把本来成立的排比强行拆散
  ▎ - 语言粗糙得不像目标读者的语境

  S4. Quality gate (06) misses "Is this interesting?"

  All five gates are structural/linguistic. You can pass all five and still produce an article nobody wants to finish reading. Gate 3 (读者门) asks "主读者为什么会继续读下去" but doesn't give diagnostics
  for boredom.

  Suggestion: Add to Gate 3 检查:

  ▎ - 读者在第二段就能猜到结尾说什么吗——如果能，这篇稿缺少推进感
  ▎ - 有没有段落在重复前面已经说过的判断，只是换了措辞

  S5. No "cold read" step between drafting and quality gate.

  The quality gate jumps straight into checking specific gates. The most natural editorial instinct — read the whole thing once as a reader, not as an editor — is absent from the workflow. Each stage is
  micro-focused but nobody ever reads the draft end-to-end without a checklist in hand.

  Suggestion: Add to 06-quality-gate.md 工作步骤, before step 1:

  ▎ 0. 先不带清单通读一遍全文。记下第一反应：哪里卡、哪里无聊、哪里想跳过。这些直觉比任何门都准。

  S6. "作者动作句" check lives only in 04-packaging but should also be in 06 or 07.

  Packaging step 6 says "删掉作者站出来解释自己在干什么的句子" with specific examples. But if you skip packaging (as we did in the 最短路径 or 编辑适配路径), this check disappears entirely. Our article had
  "这是一篇观察不是一条 claim" survive through drafting, quality-gate, and de-ai — exactly because packaging was skipped.

  Suggestion: Add a cross-reference in 07-de-ai.md 重点排查项 #4（作者动作句过多）:

  ▎ 特别注意：如果跳过了 04-packaging，这一项是你唯一的拦截点。参考 04 步骤 6 的例句。

  S7. Framing forces "只定一个主读者" but gives no guidance on the trade-off.

  The rule is correct — you should pick one. But when the user pushes back (as happened in this run: "ABC all three"), the workflow says "不能列一串" and stops. It doesn't help the writer think about what
  they sacrifice by picking one over another. The pushback isn't resolved, just overruled.

  Suggestion: Add to 01-framing.md 步骤 2:

  ▎ 如果你觉得必须覆盖多类读者：先选一个主读者写完，再检查另外两类是否能从同一篇稿里各取所需。如果不能，你需要的是多篇稿，不是一篇更长的稿。
